What We Fix

Three data problems almost every nonprofit has

1. Donor data in too many places

Your CRM, your email platform, your event tool, and a development director's spreadsheet each have a different version of the same donor. We pick a source of truth, clean the duplicates, and connect the rest so it stays that way.

2. Grant reporting that eats weeks

Funders want outcomes tied to dollars. When program data and accounting don't connect, staff rebuild the same report by hand every cycle. Data strategy for nonprofits starts here: define the outcomes once, track them consistently, and generate grant and board reports from the same source.

3. Program data nobody can analyze

Intake forms, surveys, and attendance live in Google Forms, Airtable, or paper. We bring them into a structure that can be reported on, and into Power BI or Looker Studio dashboards your team and board can read.

Questions

What nonprofits ask first

What does a nonprofit data systems consultant do?
A nonprofit data systems consultant connects the tools your organization runs on (donor CRM, accounting, program and survey tools) so data flows between them, reports can be trusted, and staff stop re-entering the same information. The goal is faster grant and board reporting and a clean foundation for AI.
We use Salesforce Nonprofit Cloud (or NPSP). Can you help?
Yes. We work with both Salesforce Nonprofit Cloud and the older NPSP, including data cleanup, reporting, integration with accounting, and planning a migration between them when that makes sense.
Is our nonprofit ready for AI?
Most nonprofits can start using AI for writing and research right away. Using AI on donor, client, or program data takes more care. We assess data quality, privacy, and tool settings first so you don't expose sensitive data or get unreliable answers.
We're a small nonprofit with a small budget. Is this realistic?
Yes. We start with a fixed-scope audit or a small retainer, use the tools you already have, and prioritize the fixes that save the most staff time. Some nonprofits fund this kind of work through capacity-building grants.
Do you only work with Seattle nonprofits?
No. We work remotely with nonprofits across the U.S. and North America, and offer hybrid and in-person sessions across the Puget Sound region, from Olympia to Bellingham.
