What this site collects, why, and how to opt out.

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Who we are

This website is run by QAMW Consulting, a systems consulting practice based in Seattle, Washington. Questions about this policy can go to info@qamwconsulting.com.

Analytics and cookies

We use Google Analytics to understand how people use this site: which pages are visited, how long people stay, roughly where visitors are located, what device and browser they use, and which buttons get clicked (for example, sharing a webinar invite). Google Analytics stores this using cookies whose names start with _ga.

We use this information only to improve the site and our content. We don't run ads on this site, we don't use advertising cookies, and we never sell your information.

When you first visit, a notice explains this. If you click Decline, Google Analytics is turned off for your browser and existing analytics cookies are removed. Your choice is saved in your browser so you won't be asked again on each page.

Information you give us

Some pages let you share information directly:

  • Bookings. Discovery calls and webinar registrations are handled by TidyCal. You give your name, email, and any answers you choose to add.
  • Forms and quizzes. Some forms, like the Skill Gap Finder, are handled by Tally. You give your name, email, organization, and your quiz answers.
  • Email. If you email us, we receive whatever you send.

We use this information to respond to you, deliver what you asked for, and follow up about our services. You can ask us to stop contacting you at any time. Those services have their own privacy policies covering how they store data.

Fonts

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers to download them. This doesn't set cookies.

Your choices

  • Decline analytics using the notice or the button above.
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings.
  • Install Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
  • Ask us to see, correct, or delete the information you've shared with us by emailing info@qamwconsulting.com.

Changes

If we change how we handle information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
