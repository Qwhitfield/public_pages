Questions

What people ask before engaging

What is a fractional data strategist?
A fractional data strategist is a senior data and systems lead who works with your company part-time or on a retainer. You get experienced judgment on your data, reporting, and tech stack without the cost of a full-time executive hire.
What does a systems audit for a small business include?
Our audit includes a systems map of your full tech stack, a data and reporting reality check, a revenue leak analysis with estimated dollar impact, an AI readiness score across five dimensions, and a prioritized 30 to 60 day execution plan.
Is AI readiness consulting worth it for a small business?
If you're paying for AI tools or planning to, yes. Readiness work makes sure the tools are working with accurate, connected data and that you're starting with tasks that save real hours, so the investment pays off instead of stalling.
How is this different from hiring a big consulting firm?
You work directly with the senior person doing the work, not a junior team staffed after the pitch. We right-size every recommendation for your budget and team, and we hand off with documentation so you're not dependent on us.
Do you work with startups outside Washington?
Yes. We work remotely with startups and small businesses across the U.S., with hybrid in-person options in Greater Seattle and the Eastside.
