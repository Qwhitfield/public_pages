On Databases & Automation
"I manage our donor database on top of everything else. I'm not a tech person. Can AI actually help me automate some of what I'm doing manually?"
We'll cover how AI fits into CRM and database workflows — what it can help with now, what still needs human hands, and how to think about automation when you don't have a dedicated tech person on staff.
On Getting Started
"All five of us have used AI personally but we don't have an org-level account or a policy. Where do we even start?"
We'll walk through exactly this — how to move from scattered personal use to something intentional, shared, and actually useful for your team's daily work.
On Reporting
"Impact Reporting and Data Storytelling is top of my list. We've already talked AI strategy — now I want to actually do something with our data."
We're going hands-on with this. Not theory — actual ways to use AI to turn program data and community feedback into something a funder or board can act on.
On Program Documentation
"Knowledge management is a real gap for us. Stuff lives in people's heads. How do we change that without adding more work?"
We'll look at how AI can help you capture, organize, and make institutional knowledge accessible — without turning documentation into another project nobody has time for.
