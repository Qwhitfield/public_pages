What "IT consultant" means here

When people in Sammamish or Bellevue search for an IT consultant, they usually mean one of two things. If you need help with laptops, networks, Wi-Fi, or security patches, you want a managed IT provider, and we're happy to point you to good ones.

If your problem is that your CRM, accounting, and project tools don't share data, your reports don't match, or your team does the same manual work every week, that's what we do. We focus on the data and operations layer: the systems that run your business and the numbers your leadership reads.

Yes. Power BI is one of our deepest specialties, including dashboard design, semantic modeling, DAX, automated refresh, and team training. We work with Seattle-area organizations in person and remotely.
