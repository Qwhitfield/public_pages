The Work

What data strategy means at your size

At a large company, "data strategy" means a team, a warehouse, and a multi-year roadmap. At a 15 to 100 person business or nonprofit, it means something more practical: everyone agrees on what the key numbers are, where they come from, and how often they update. Then those numbers show up somewhere useful without someone rebuilding a spreadsheet every Monday.

We've built reporting at enterprise scale, including Power BI templates deployed through Microsoft Viva Insights to over 500 enterprise clients. We bring that discipline down to a size that fits your budget and your team.

Signs you need a data strategy, not another report

  • Two people pull the same number and get two different answers.
  • Your board or leadership report takes days to assemble.
  • You have a Power BI or Looker Studio dashboard nobody trusts.
  • Revenue in your CRM never matches revenue in QuickBooks.
  • You want to use AI on your data but don't know if it's clean enough.

Questions

What people ask before engaging

What does a data strategy consultant do for a small business?
A data strategy consultant helps you decide which numbers matter, makes sure they come from a trusted source, and builds the reporting that delivers them automatically. For a small business that usually means KPI definitions, connected systems, and a few well-built dashboards rather than a large data platform.
Do we need a data warehouse?
Usually not at first. Many SMBs and nonprofits get what they need from a well-designed Power BI semantic model connected directly to their CRM and accounting tools. We recommend a warehouse only when data volume or the number of sources justifies it.
Can you fix our existing Power BI reports?
Yes. A lot of our work starts with an existing Power BI, Tableau, or Looker Studio report that nobody trusts. We audit the model, fix the measures and data sources, and document it so the problem doesn't come back.
Power BI, Tableau, or Looker Studio?
If you're on Microsoft 365, Power BI is usually the best value and the easiest to maintain. Google Workspace teams often start with Looker Studio. We'll recommend based on your stack, budget, and who will maintain it.
Do you offer ongoing or fractional data strategy?
Yes. After an audit or a scoped build, some clients keep us on as a fractional data and operations lead. See the startup and SMB page for how that works.
