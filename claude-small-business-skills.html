Before you start

What owners ask on the way in.

Most people find the finder while trying to get AI to work with their books, payroll, or reports. Here's what they want to know first.

Can Claude actually connect to my QuickBooks and Gusto?

Yes. The September 15 release added 27 integrations. QuickBooks, Xero, Gusto, Stripe, Square, Shopify, and HubSpot are on the list. The finder shows which skills plug into the tools you check.

Will it change my books or run payroll without me?

The skills in this release are built to prepare the work and wait for your yes. Plan Payroll stages the run and never presses the button. AP Processor stages bill entries for your approval. You stay the one who signs off.

I already tried ChatGPT with my spreadsheets. Why is this different?

A pasted export is a snapshot with no context. These skills read from the system itself and follow the same routine every time.

One catch. AI is only as right as the data under it. Messy books go in, confident messy answers come out.

My tools don't agree with each other. Will AI fix that?

No. AI reads what's there. When QuickBooks says one number and your job software says another, a summary will just pick one. Someone has to find that gap first. That's the work Quinita does in a Systems Reality Check.

Do I need to be technical to use any of this?

No. You ask for things in plain words, like “close the month” or “give me my Monday brief.” The finder itself is three multiple choice questions.

We run mostly on spreadsheets. Is this still for us?

Yes. Skip the tool list in step 2. Nearly every skill works from an upload or a pasted sheet.

Do I have to buy new software?

No. The finder only matches skills to what you already have. Getting more out of the tools you own comes first. Running the skills does need Claude itself.

We're a nonprofit. Does this apply to us?

Yes. Pick nonprofit in step 1. A few questions shift to fit donors, grants, and programs.

What happens after I see my results?
