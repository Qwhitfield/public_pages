The audit is the starting point — you own the roadmap and can take it to any team. If you want me to stay involved for implementation, we can discuss a follow-on engagement. Most clients move forward with me on either a project basis or an ongoing fractional systems role.
What industries do you work with?
I work with $1M+ growing businesses and nonprofits across various industries. While their services differ, the operational headache is almost always the same: software that doesn't talk to each other, hours wasted on manual admin work, and reports leadership can't fully trust. Grounded in 14+ years of enterprise operations, data, and systems integration, I focus on fixing your backend foundation and optimizing the tools you already own—no matter what industry you're in.
