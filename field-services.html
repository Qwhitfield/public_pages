Where Jobs Slip

Five handoffs where jobs fall through the cracks

Every job moves through the same handoffs. Each one is a spot where work can disappear when no person or system owns the next step.

1
Handoff 01
Lead comes in
A call rings while the owner is on a roof. A web form lands at 9pm. A homeshow lead goes on a paper sign-up sheet. None of them reach your CRM, so none of them get a call back.
2
Handoff 02
Lead becomes a booked estimate
The lead is in the system, but booking takes three rounds of phone tag. By the time you call back, they've booked someone else.
3
Handoff 03
Estimate becomes a signed job
The bid goes out. Everyone means to follow up. The week gets busy. The job goes to the contractor who did follow up.
4
Handoff 04
Office hands off to the field
The schedule lives in one place and the job notes live in another. Techs show up missing details. Jobs get pushed. Customers get frustrated.
5
Handoff 05
Finished job becomes paid revenue
The work is done, but the invoice waits on someone to move it into QuickBooks. Nobody asks for a review. Revenue you already earned leaks out the back door.

Usually not. Most field service companies already own the tools they need. The gaps come from how those tools are set up and how work moves between them. We start with what you have.

ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, Buildertrend, HubSpot, QuickBooks, Power BI, Excel, Google Workspace, and Microsoft 365. Other tools are fine too. The work is about the handoffs between tools, not one product.

The Systems Reality Check takes 14 days. Simple fixes like a missed-call text back can go live within a week after that. Bigger integrations are scoped with clear timelines.

No. QAMW is based in South Seattle and works with field service companies anywhere in the US. All work can be done remotely.
